## Credit and Citation

The licence has two layers on attribution.

1. **Required Credit (licence section 5.4).** Whenever you copy, quote, share, translate, adapt or present any OpenVAPT Material (code, documentation, Academy modules, worksheets, check descriptions, report templates, website text, figures or screenshots), in any medium, you must credit OpenVAPT: name "OpenVAPT", give openvapt.com or the GitHub address, give the version where you know it, and say if you adapted it. Any normal academic citation does this. Credit may be given in any reasonable way for the medium, and at the first practical place where it does not fit at the point of use. It must not suggest endorsement.
2. **Requested citation.** For running the tool, reporting its results, or using ideas and knowledge learned from it, no Credit is legally required, because copyright does not protect ideas, facts or methods and a licence cannot change fair dealing under the Copyright Act 1987. We still ask you to cite OpenVAPT, and academic integrity rules usually require it.

Ready-to-copy citations in every common style are in `CITING.md`; `CITATION.cff` is the machine-readable form.

## Why We Say Source-Available

The source code is fully public, for everyone to read and review, and you may modify it within the licence. The licence restricts use to Own Use, so OpenVAPT is not "open source" under the Open Source Initiative's definition. The accurate term is **source-available**. We use "open" on this website only in its plain sense: nothing about the code is hidden.
