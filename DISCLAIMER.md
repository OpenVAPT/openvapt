## Automated Results Can Be Wrong

OpenVAPT runs automated, read-only checks. Results may include false positives (reported problems that are not real) and false negatives (real problems that are missed). **Absence of findings is not proof of security.** Application logic flaws and many other weaknesses require manual testing by qualified professionals.
