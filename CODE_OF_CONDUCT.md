# Code of Conduct

OpenVAPT adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome learners of every level, and keep discussion on the project. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing others' private information are not accepted.

Because OpenVAPT has no email contact, conduct concerns are raised through GitHub's built-in "Report content" feature on the comment or issue concerned. Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation for violations.
