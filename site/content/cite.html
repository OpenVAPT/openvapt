How to Cite OpenVAPT

Please cite us. If OpenVAPT, its Academy, its documentation, its checks or its reports helped your work in any way, in a research paper, thesis, dissertation, coursework, assignment, presentation, poster, academic project, teaching material, book, article, blog post or video, please cite OpenVAPT.

This page follows Citing OpenVAPT (CITING.md), the canonical guide. If anything here differs, CITING.md governs.

{{icon "scale"}}

Credit Is Required When You Copy

Section 5.4 of the OpenVAPT Source-Available Licence 1.3 requires Credit whenever you copy, quote, share, translate, adapt or present any OpenVAPT Material, in whole or in part, in any medium: code, documentation, Academy modules, quizzes, worksheets, check descriptions, report templates, website text, figures, diagrams and screenshots.

Credit names "OpenVAPT", gives openvapt.com or the GitHub address, gives the version where you know it, says "adapted from" or "translated from" where you changed the material, and never suggests endorsement. Any citation on this page does all of that.

{{icon "quote"}}

Citation Is Requested When You Use It

We also ask you to cite OpenVAPT when you only ran the tool, used its findings, or used ideas, methods or knowledge you learned from it without copying its words, code or figures. This is a request, not a licence condition, and nothing limits fair dealing or any other right the law gives you.

Academic integrity rules at almost every university and journal require you to acknowledge the tools and sources you used, so citing OpenVAPT is usually expected of you anyway.

{{icon "code-xml"}} Cite a Specific Version and Commit

Use the year of the version you used and the version shown by openvapt version or on the release page. Pre-release versions (alpha, beta, rc) are fine to cite; give the full version string. For unreleased code, or to make your work exactly reproducible, add the commit: get it with git rev-parse HEAD, or from the footer of this website ("Built ... from commit"). This build is commit {{.CommitShort}}.

{{icon "shield-check"}} Use Does Not Imply Endorsement

Citing OpenVAPT does not give you any permission the licence does not give, and it does not mean OpenVAPT endorses, approves or has reviewed your work. Do not describe your work as certified, audited or approved by OpenVAPT. OpenVAPT findings are not a professional assessment; describe them as results from an academic tool, used at your own risk.
