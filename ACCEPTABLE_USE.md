# Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.4, effective 2026-10-13. Drafted by an AI agent and reviewed by an AI agent acting as the project's legal reviewer; no human lawyer drafted or reviewed it.

OpenVAPT is for academic, learning and Own Use only. It is not a business and must never be used to provide services.

## Allowed

- Reading, studying and discussing the source code and documentation.
- Downloading OpenVAPT to your own server and running it there for personal testing and learning.
- Running OpenVAPT to test your own company's servers, on machines your company owns or controls and has authorised you to assess.
- Users under 18 may use OpenVAPT only with a parent's or guardian's agreement.
- Running `openvapt demo` against the simulated lab for your own learning.
- Free Teaching, as defined in the licence: showing the source code and running OpenVAPT against its simulated lab, or on machines the teacher or the educational institution owns or controls and has authorised. Each student accepts the EULA personally before downloading or running it.
- Using pre-release builds (alpha, beta, rc), which may be wrong or unstable, only at your own risk and preferably on a non-production machine.
- Modifying the code for your own learning or own use, and contributing improvements back.

## Not Allowed

- Running OpenVAPT on any machine that you or your employer do not own or control, including a client's or customer's machine, even with their permission, or on your employer's machine without your employer's authorisation.
- Modifying OpenVAPT, or reusing its code, to examine, access, attack or disrupt systems you are not authorised to examine, including removing or weakening the scope guard for that purpose.
- Providing any service to anyone else with OpenVAPT or with anything from or produced by it, paid or unpaid, such as vulnerability assessments, penetration tests, audits, scanning for clients, security consulting, a hosted or managed service, or selling or supplying reports.
- Selling, renting or licensing OpenVAPT, or including it in a product, platform or service for others.
- Presenting OpenVAPT reports as a certification, a professional penetration test, or an endorsement by OpenVAPT, or calling a modified version OpenVAPT without clearly saying it is modified.
- Running OpenVAPT on students' machines for them, or using it in any course, workshop or training run by a commercial training provider.
- Using OpenVAPT in breach of any law.
