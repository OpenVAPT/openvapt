# Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.1, effective 2026-10-13. Drafted by AI and reviewed by an AI legal reviewer; awaiting review by a licensed Malaysian advocate and solicitor.

OpenVAPT is for academic, learning and Own Use only. It is not a business and must never be used to provide services.

## Allowed

- Reading, studying and discussing the source code and documentation.
- Running OpenVAPT on computers and servers you own or control, for your own use, including inside your own company on machines your company owns or controls and has authorised you to assess.
- Users under 18 may use OpenVAPT only with a parent's or guardian's agreement.
- Running `openvapt demo` against the simulated lab for learning and teaching.
- Modifying the code for your own learning or own use, and contributing improvements back.

## Not Allowed

- Running OpenVAPT on any machine that you or your employer do not own or control, including a client's or customer's machine, even with their permission, or on your employer's machine without your employer's authorisation.
- Modifying OpenVAPT, or reusing its code, to examine, access, attack or disrupt systems you are not authorised to examine, including removing or weakening the scope guard for that purpose.
- Providing services to others with OpenVAPT, paid or unpaid, such as vulnerability assessments, penetration tests, audits, scans or security consulting.
- Selling, renting or licensing OpenVAPT, or including it in a product, platform or service for others.
- Presenting OpenVAPT reports as a certification, a professional penetration test, or an endorsement by the OpenVAPT Project, or calling a modified version OpenVAPT without clearly saying it is modified.
- Using OpenVAPT in breach of any law.

## Consequences

Using OpenVAPT outside this policy is outside your licence, which then ends as described in the licence. You alone are responsible for any consequences.
